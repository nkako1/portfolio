Lamps

Lamps and light sculptures, shaped by hand from paper, reed, and found objects in Brooklyn.

  • Knot — pendant lamp, mulberry paper and reed
  • Flame — table lamp, mulberry paper and reed
  • Scallop — table lamp, mulberry paper on a found wire frame
  • Oyster — wall light, mulberry paper and reed
  • Cloud — pendant lamp, mulberry paper and reed
  • Bug — floor lamp, mulberry paper and reed
  • Cone — pendant lamp, mulberry paper and reed

Every piece is made by hand in a tent in my backyard in Brooklyn, also known as “the bubble.”
